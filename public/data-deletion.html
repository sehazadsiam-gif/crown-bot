🛡️ User Data Deletion Instructions

Data Deletion Request Guide

In compliance with Meta Platform Terms • Updated September 2026

Under the Meta Platform Terms and global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), users have the right to request the deletion of any personal data, identifiers, and conversation histories collected during interactions with our chatbot applications across Facebook Messenger and Instagram.

If you have interacted with any business page powered by Crown Bot and wish to permanently delete your data from our database, please choose one of the following methods:

Options to Delete Your Data

1 Option 1: Instant In-Chat Request (Fastest)

You can trigger data deletion directly from the Facebook Messenger or Instagram Direct conversation thread:

  1. Open the chat thread with the Facebook Page or Instagram profile.
  2. Send a message containing the phrase: Delete my data or Remove my history.
  3. Our system will flag your account for purge and confirm the deletion request within the chat thread.
2 Option 2: Email Data Deletion Request

You can send an email to our data privacy team:

  • Send an email to: admin@ccadmin.online
  • Subject: Meta Platform Data Deletion Request
  • Include: Your Facebook / Instagram display name, the Facebook Page or Instagram profile you messaged, and approximate date of conversation.
  • Our administrators will query the database, wipe all corresponding message logs, and send you a written confirmation of deletion within 24 to 48 hours.
3 Option 3: Remove Permissions via Facebook Settings

To disconnect your Facebook account from our app entirely:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile > Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Select Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate the business assistant app and click Remove.
  4. Facebook will notify our webhook system to dissociate any linked tokens.

What Data Is Deleted?

Upon receiving and processing a deletion request, we permanently delete:

  • Your Page-Scoped User ID (PSID) and Instagram Scoped ID (IGSID).
  • Your public display name and any profile metadata retrieved from Meta.
  • All incoming and outgoing message records, transcripts, and timestamps.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.
