Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 • Effective Date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crown Bot ("we", "us", "our", or the "Platform") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you interact with our automated messaging assistants and chat applications across Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct, WhatsApp, TikTok, and web chat widgets connected to our platform.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all end-users who send messages, inquiries, booking requests, or orders to any business Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp business number, or web chat widget operated with our automated customer messaging service.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide real-time conversational responses and facilitate customer service for the connected business:

  • Platform-Provided User Identifiers: Anonymous page-scoped user IDs (e.g. Meta Page-Scoped ID PSID, Instagram Scoped ID IGSID) provided securely by Meta Graph API or corresponding platforms. We do not receive or store your personal Facebook or Instagram login passwords or private personal data.
  • Public Profile Details: Your publicly available display name or profile name as returned by the platform's API to address you politely in chat.
  • Message Contents: Text messages, inquiries, preferences, orders, appointment booking details, or phone numbers that you voluntarily type in the chat conversation.
  • Timestamps & Message Metadata: Date, time, and status of messages for conversation continuity and quality assurance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate business communication purposes:

  • To generate immediate, 24/7 automated answers to your questions regarding opening hours, services, menus, prices, locations, and facilities.
  • To record and relay booking, reservation, or order inquiries to the business owner or staff for fulfillment.
  • To notify human staff when an inquiry requires human escalation or personal assistance.
  • To maintain a continuous conversation context so you do not have to repeat yourself when speaking with the assistant or human agent.
🚫 Zero Data Commercialization

We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data. We do not share your information with data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing services.

4. AI Processing & Sub-processors

To understand natural language queries and generate accurate responses, message text is processed through secure, enterprise-grade AI APIs (such as Google Gemini API or Groq). These AI services process conversation turns ephemerally to return a relevant reply and do not retain your private messages for commercial ad targeting.

5. Data Storage and Security

All conversation records are stored in a dedicated, secure, encrypted database hosted in enterprise data centers. We implement industry-standard security measures, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification for all incoming platform webhooks from Meta.
  • Role-based access control ensuring only authorized business owners can review their customer inbox threads.

6. Data Retention

We retain conversation histories only for as long as necessary to provide customer support, verify order/booking records, and resolve inquiries. Inactive conversations are automatically archived or purged according to the business workspace retention schedule.

7. User Data Deletion Instructions

You have the full right to request the immediate deletion of your conversation history, name, and platform identifiers from our system at any time.

🗑️ How to Request Data Deletion

You can request data removal at any time using any of the following methods:

  1. Direct in Chat: Simply send a message to the Facebook Page or Instagram profile saying: Delete my data or Remove my history.
  2. Email Support: Send an email to admin@ccadmin.online with your Facebook/Instagram display name and the Page you messaged.
  3. Via Meta Settings: Visit your Facebook/Instagram App Settings and remove the App permissions.

For full details, visit our dedicated User Data Deletion Instructions page. Requests are processed within 24–48 hours.
